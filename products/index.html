“Available” means it runs today and you can buy it, and four of the eight are: ShareCare, SecurityPortal, WebScan and MailTrust. “Coming soon” means built and running but not on sale. “Under development” means not yet available. Where a plane is detected but not yet remediable, the product’s own page says so — and the limits page collects every one of these in one place, because we would rather label it plainly than let you find out in month two.

Consent is the part that is not shared

Each product that reads your tenant has its own Entra application and asks for its own grant, scoped to what that product needs. So adding a product is a second consent — but never a second integration project, and no product carries permissions it has no use for. Two products need no grant at all: WebScan works entirely from outside, and PosturePortal reads what the others already wrote. What each consent does and does not cover.

What actually gets crawled

Every surface below that sits inside a tenant or a cloud account is read app-only, on a schedule, and scored into one findings store; the public web and email-authentication surfaces are read from outside, with no grant at all. Dashed means detected but not yet remediable app-only — the distinction is stated rather than glossed.

How billing works

No surprises, and no hard stops

  • Scans, findings, remediation and evidence exports are not metered.
  • Over your units: a warning, then a banner, trued up on the next invoice. A scan is never stopped for a commercial reason.
  • ShareCare’s volume bands apply to the whole bill, not just the users past a threshold.
  • No suite discount and no annual-billing discount: the price you see is the price.
The portfolio

Four running today, four on the way

Each one answers a question you are probably already being asked, is sold on its own, and the scanning ones feed the same shared findings store. SecurityPortal finds the weaknesses, CompliancePortal turns them into evidence and PosturePortal puts the picture on one board — two of those three are not released yet, so that loop is not closed until they ship.

Available today

Data access governance

ShareCare Available

What is shared outside, what is over-shared inside so Copilot can reach it, and who can fix it. Crawls seven Microsoft 365 planes app-only, plus Box and Slack Connect, scores by sensitivity and blast radius, and remediates with a grace window and undo.

ShareCare
Posture scanning

SecurityPortal Available

Continuous Microsoft 365 and Entra posture — Conditional Access coverage, MFA enforcement gaps, over-permissioned apps, risky sign-in patterns. Findings carry control tags so they land in CompliancePortal as evidence.

SecurityPortal
External attack surface

WebScan Available

Finds and grades the public face of your organisation — subdomain discovery, certificate transparency, TLS, headers, cookies, DNS, exposed services and infrastructure. Needs no tenant and no consent, and the scan itself is free on every tenant; you pay to keep the results.

WebScan
Email authentication

MailTrust Available

SPF, DKIM, DMARC, BIMI and MTA-STS posture, real sender inventory from aggregate reports, a staged path to enforcement, and DNS records written in-product for supported providers.

MailTrust

Built, and not released yet

Control evidence

CompliancePortal Coming soon

Maps the findings you already produce onto the frameworks you care about, with attestation for the controls that cannot be automated, an evidence repository and time-boxed auditor access. Technical control evidence — not a whole-company compliance programme.

CompliancePortal
Policy governance

ConditionalAccessPortal Coming soon

Conditional Access policy inventory and metering, baseline coverage gaps, and approval-gated policy write-back — on the Visibility / Governance / Automation ladder. Its Entra app asks for read scope only until write-back is consented separately. Not released yet.

Single pane

PosturePortal Under development

Read-only by design. Aggregates findings, risk scores, trends, connector health and coverage from every product into one per-tenant and fleet-wide board, with saved views and annotations for client and board reporting.

Drift governance

Dredd Under development

Your approved configuration as a versioned baseline. Every deviation becomes a finding with two answers: revert it, or ratify it into the baseline. Both are recorded, attributed and versioned.
